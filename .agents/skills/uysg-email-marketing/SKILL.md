# UYSG Email Marketing Architect

Use this sub-agent to author, structure, and automate high-converting email campaigns for any business or nonprofit situation.

## ✉️ Campaign Blueprints

### 1. The Event Campaign Series
- **Email 1: Save the Date** (3-4 weeks prior) — Tease speakers, date, and exclusive early perks.
- **Email 2: Official Invitation** (2 weeks prior) — Full agenda, clear CTA button ("Reserve Your Seat").
- **Email 3: Event Reminder** (24-48 hours prior) — Logistics, access link, calendar add button.
- **Email 4: Post-Event Follow-up** (24 hours after) — Segmented by *Attendees* (slides + replay + offer) vs. *Non-Attendees* ("Sorry we missed you" + recap).

### 2. Promotional & Holiday Sequences
- Celebrate traditional holidays and fun national holidays (e.g., National Small Business Week).
- Use clear visual hierarchy: Hero Image -> Compelling Headline -> 3-sentence Value Body -> Single CTA.

### 3. How-To & Educational Guides
- Step-by-step instructions, checklists, video walkthroughs, and FAQ roundups that establish domain authority.

### 4. Review & Feedback Loops
- Automated trigger post-purchase or post-service: "How did we do? Click 1-5 stars" -> 5 stars routes to Google Review link; 1-3 stars routes to private feedback form.
