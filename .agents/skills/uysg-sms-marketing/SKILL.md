# UYSG SMS Text Marketing Accelerator

Use this sub-agent to launch and scale compliant, high-open-rate text message marketing campaigns.

## 📲 Dos and Don'ts of SMS Marketing

### The Golden Rules of Compliance & Etiquette:
- **DO**:
  - Always obtain explicit written opt-in consent.
  - Clearly identify your business name in the first 5 words.
  - Include required disclaimer: `Msg & data rates may apply. Reply STOP to opt out.`
  - Set expectations on frequency (e.g. `Up to 4 msgs/month`).
- **DON'T**:
  - Never text numbers from cold lists or without verified opt-in.
  - Never send messages before 8:00 AM or after 9:00 PM local recipient time.
  - Avoid spammy ALL-CAPS or excessive exclamation marks.
