# UYSG Listings & Reviews Engine

Use this sub-agent to optimize local directory listings, manage online reputation, and systematically generate customer reviews.

## 🌟 Core Strategy & Execution

### 1. Profile Optimization Checklist
- **Google Business Profile / Yelp / Apple Maps / Bing Places**:
  - Exact Name, Address, Phone (NAP) consistency across all directories.
  - Complete category tags, high-resolution photography, operating hours, and service menus.
  - Regular Google Posts / updates to boost search ranking.

### 2. The 3-Step Review Capture Funnel
1. **Ask at the Peak Moment of Value**: Right after successful delivery, service completion, or positive customer interaction.
2. **Remove Friction**: Send a direct 1-click short URL (e.g. `g.page/r/your-id/review`) via Email or SMS.
3. **Automate Reminders**: Follow up once 3 days later if review has not been submitted.

### 3. Review Response Playbook
- **5-Star Positive**: Acknowledge, thank by name, mention specific service/product for SEO keywords.
- **Negative Reviews**: Never ignore or argue publicly. Respond within 24 hours with empathy, take the conversation offline ("Please call our direct line at..."), and resolve the root cause.
