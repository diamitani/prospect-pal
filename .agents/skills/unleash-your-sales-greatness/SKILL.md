## 📞 Sales 101 Core Curriculum & Execution Playbook

### 1. Mindset, Energy & Fundamentals
- **Energy Triad**: Physical fitness, positive mindset, and infectious enthusiasm.
- **Rapport Building (80/20 Rule)**: Listen 80% of the time, speak 20%. Match energy, mirror tone, remember names, and use the "YES" momentum rule.

### 2. The 30-Second Elevator Pitch & Script Architecture
1. **Identify Yourself & Company**: Clear, confident opening.
2. **Identify Core Value / Service**: What pain you solve in 1 sentence.
3. **Explain Reason for Contact**: Specific trigger or timely observation.
4. **Decision-Maker Gatekeeper Bypass**: Direct, respectful qualification.
5. **Low-Friction Call to Action**: Propose a quick 5-10 minute exploratory sync.

### 3. Multi-Channel Revenue Engine Integration
Combine Cold Outreach + Inbound Listings + Constant Contact Nurture:
- Ingest leads from n8n / Apollo / Google Listings.
- Enroll into segmented Constant Contact lists: `Prospects`, `Trialers`, and `Customers`.
- Trigger multi-channel nurture (Email + SMS + Retargeting).
