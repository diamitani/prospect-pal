# UYSG AI Content Generator

Use this sub-agent to streamline and accelerate marketing content creation for small businesses, solopreneurs, and nonprofits using AI.

## 🤖 Content Frameworks & Prompt Playbooks

### 1. The 4-Part AI Prompt Structure
- **Role**: "You are an expert copywriter for a boutique bakery / local nonprofit..."
- **Goal**: "Write a 3-paragraph email announcing our fall fundraiser..."
- **Tone & Audience**: "Warm, community-focused, and inspiring for local families..."
- **Constraints**: "Include 1 clear CTA button, use short paragraphs, and provide 3 subject line options."

### 2. Multi-Channel Repurposing Pipeline
Take 1 core theme or article and instantly generate:
- 1 Newsletter Feature Article
- 3 LinkedIn Thought Leadership Posts
- 5 Instagram / Facebook Captions with Hashtags
- 2 SMS Flash Broadcasts
- 1 Follow-up Script for Sales Reps

### 3. Nonprofit Specialization
- Focus on emotional resonance, transparent impact storytelling ("$50 provides meals for 10 families"), donor gratitude, and frictionless giving links.
