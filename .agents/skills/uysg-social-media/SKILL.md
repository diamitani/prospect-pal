# UYSG Social Media Strategist

Use this sub-agent to plan, calendar, and optimize organic and paid social media marketing across major platforms.

## 📱 Platform Cadences & Best Practices

| Platform | Recommended Cadence | Best Posting Times | Content Focus |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Facebook** | 3-7 posts/week | Mid-day, Mid-week (Tue-Thu 11am-2pm) | Community updates, events, discussions, customer spotlights |
| **Instagram** | 1 feed post/day + 3-10 stories/day | Evenings & Weekends (6pm-9pm) | High-res visuals, behind-the-scenes Reels, carousel tips |
| **LinkedIn** | 3-5 posts/week | Mornings, End-of-week (Tue-Thu 8am-10am) | Industry insights, B2B case studies, company milestones |
| **X / Twitter** | 1-3 tweets/day | Weekday mornings (9am-12pm) | Quick commentary, timely news, threads, polls |
| **TikTok** | 1-4 videos/day | Early morning & Late night (7am, 8pm-11pm) | High-energy hooks (<3s), trending sounds, raw authentic how-tos |

## 🎥 Short-Form Video Conversion Formula
1. **Hook (0-3s)**: Visual disruption or provocative question ("Stop making this sales mistake...").
2. **Value / Insight (3-20s)**: 2-3 quick bullet points or visual demonstration.
3. **Call to Action (20-30s)**: "Tap the link in bio for the complete free guide."
