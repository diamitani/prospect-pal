# Prospect PAL Landing Page Brief

## Interview Answers

### 1. Vibe (3-5 words)
**Premium, precise, engineered**

References:
- Stripe's technical confidence
- Linear's product clarity  
- The feeling of a well-architected system you'd trust with your revenue pipeline

### 2. Scroll Journey
Sales funnel optimized: landing → understanding → desire → purchase.

**Beats:**
1. **Recognition** — They see their current reality: manual GTM chaos, hours lost to research
2. **Promise** — The vision: one automated engine that does it all
3. **Build** (PEAK) — The 9-node workflow wires itself before their eyes as they scroll
4. **Power** — What this engine unlocks: time back, pipeline growth, precision at scale
5. **Proof** — Signal intelligence: live leads that already run n8n, ready to contact
6. **Commitment** — Three pricing tiers, transparent, "Build my engine" CTA

### 3. Energy Curve
- **Recognition**: Calm, relatable
- **Promise**: Rising, possibility
- **Build**: PEAK INTENSITY — tactile, satisfying, "I'm building this right now"
- **Power**: Confident, expansive
- **Proof**: Concrete, credible
- **Commitment**: Decisive, clear

### 4. Feeling Curve

| Beat | Emotion | What Causes It |
|------|---------|----------------|
| Recognition | Tired recognition | Copy that names their manual grind plainly: "You research prospects. You write emails. You check CRM for dupes. Every. Single. Day." |
| Promise | Possibility opening | The headline "One engine. Everything automated." lands with weight |
| Build (PEAK) | Tactile satisfaction | Their scroll literally wires each node into the canvas. They're building their own engine. Each node clicks into place with visual/micro-interaction feedback |
| Power | Imagined freedom | "While you sleep, it runs" — copy that puts them in the future where this engine works for them |
| Proof | Credibility & FOMO | Real company names, real funding rounds, real hiring signals. "These leads exist right now." |
| Commitment | Decisive clarity | Three tiers, no confusion. "Build my engine" button is the only path forward |

**The peak**: Act 3 (Build). It gets the most scroll room (3-4 viewport heights), the silence before it (Act 2 ends clean), and the signature move (scroll-to-wire).

### 5. The ONE Moment They Remember
**"I scrolled and built my own automation engine."**

The tactile, participatory nature of watching each node wire itself as they control the scroll. Not a demo they watch — a machine they assemble.

### 6. Signature Move
**Scroll-to-Wire Workflow Canvas**

As the user scrolls through Act 3 (Build), the 9-node workflow progressively constructs itself on a fixed canvas:
- Start: Empty canvas with faint grid
- Scroll position 1: Node 1 (Intake & cron) fades in, positions itself
- Scroll position 2: Connection line animates from Node 1 → Node 2 appears
- Scroll positions 3-9: Each subsequent node wires in with connecting bezier curves
- Final position: Complete 9-node graph, fully wired, glowing with "READY TO DEPLOY" badge

The scroll is the timeline. They're not watching a video — they're the one building it, frame by frame.

**Conversion mechanic**: When the 9th node completes and the graph is wired, continuing to scroll reveals the pricing section. The transition line: "Your engine is ready. Choose your plan."

### 7. Aesthetic Range
**Premium-minimal**

Clean, restrained, high-craft. Precision typography, generous white space (or dark space), intentional color usage (likely dark theme given current brand). Matches the "engineered" vibe.

### 8. World Structure  
**Distinct scenes with clear progression** (filmic chapters)

Each beat is a clear chapter optimized for conversion:
- Crisp transitions between sections
- Each section has one job in the funnel
- No ambiguity about progression toward purchase

### 9. Assets Available
- Logo and brand colors from existing site
- Need to generate: workflow visualization assets, node iconography, any environmental/mood imagery

---

## Completion Criteria

**The page succeeds when:**
- [ ] Scroll feels tactile and participatory, not passive
- [ ] The 9-node workflow builds smoothly under scroll control (no jank)
- [ ] Visitor can clearly articulate: "I scroll, it builds my engine, I buy it"
- [ ] Peak moment (Act 3: Build) has the most scroll room and strongest visual change
- [ ] Every section serves the funnel: Recognition → Promise → Build → Power → Proof → Commitment
- [ ] Pricing is clear, no confusion about next action
- [ ] The signature move (scroll-to-wire) exists nowhere else

## Tell-Someone Sentence
"It's the site where **you scroll to build your own automation engine, and by the time it's wired, you want to buy it.**"

## Authored Silence
None. Every scroll position changes something on screen — no dead scroll.

The one exception: a brief hold at the end of Act 3 after Node 9 completes, before revealing pricing. A beat of "your engine is ready" before asking for purchase.
